# 1. Recommended Pricing Structure

The revised structure adds one entry tier — the **PTTR Reserve™ Emergency Response Membership** — beneath the existing labor-hour plans. Nothing about Recruit through Commander changes. All rates below form a single, internally consistent ladder: the more a client commits, the lower their effective labor rate and the higher their queue priority.

## 1.1 Rate ladder (the backbone of the whole program)

| Status | Hourly labor rate | Emergency dispatch fee (business hours) |
|---|---|---|
| Nonmember | $95/hr | $125 |
| Reserve member (Emergency Response Membership) | $85/hr | $50 |
| Plan subscriber (Recruit–Commander) | Included hours, then $75/hr overage | $50 |

## 1.2 Nonmember service (pay-as-you-go)

- **Standard scheduled service call:** $95/hr labor, 1-hour minimum, no dispatch fee. Scheduled to next open slot, typically 3–5 business days out.
- **Nonmember emergency call:** $125 emergency dispatch fee + $95/hr labor (1-hour minimum). Same-day or next-day response **as crew availability allows** — nonmembers are served after all members.
- **After-hours/weekend nonmember emergency:** $175 dispatch + $125/hr labor. **Holidays:** $225 dispatch + $125/hr.
- Materials billed at cost plus applicable tax. Subcontractors billed at cost + 15% coordination fee.

## 1.3 PTTR Reserve™ — Emergency Response Membership (NEW)

**Recommended price: $129/month.** (Working range considered: $99–$149. $99 sits too close to two nonmember dispatch fees and invites join-and-cancel abuse; $149 crowds the psychological gap below Recruit. $129 covers itself after roughly two service events per quarter and keeps a clean $136 gap to Recruit.)

**What it is:** contractor-on-call access and priority emergency response for hosts who can't yet forecast bookings well enough to commit to monthly labor hours. **No labor hours are included** — that is what the Recruit-and-up plans are for.

Included benefits:

- **Priority dispatch** — Reserve members are queued ahead of all nonmembers (Captain and Commander retain top-of-queue).
- **Same-day or next-available response target** — requests received by 12:00 PM are targeted for same-business-day dispatch, subject to service area, crew availability, and site safety. This is a response commitment, not a completion guarantee.
- **Member emergency dispatch fee: $50** (vs. $125 nonmember) — same fee plan subscribers pay.
- **Preferred labor rate: $85/hr** (vs. $95 nonmember), 1-hour minimum per visit.
- **Rate lock after hours** — the $85/hr labor rate does not increase evenings or weekends; only the after-hours dispatch fee applies ($95 evenings/weekends, $145 holidays).
- **Guest-damage response** — documented condition reports with time-stamped photos suitable for Airbnb AirCover / Resolution Center claims, included with every emergency visit.
- **Incident coordination across trades** — plumbing, electrical, drywall, doors, locks, appliances, HVAC, and exterior incidents. PTTR performs work within handyman scope and coordinates licensed specialty or subcontractor trades when required (sub work billed at cost + 15%).
- **Two property walkthroughs per year** — a 30-minute documented readiness inspection (photos + punch list) every six months, ideal before peak booking seasons. Walkthroughs do not accumulate or roll over.
- **Emergency stabilization first** — water shutoff, circuit isolation, board-up, tarping, securing doors/locks. Stabilization happens on the emergency visit; full permanent repair is quoted and scheduled as follow-up work.

Terms:

- **No rollover.** Reserve includes access and benefits, not banked hours — nothing accrues.
- **Minimum commitment: 3 months** (consistent with all PTTR plans), then month-to-month.
- **Cancellation:** 30 days' written notice after the initial term. No refund of partial months. Membership benefits apply only while the account is current.
- **Materials** billed separately at cost plus applicable tax; **subcontractors** at cost + 15%.
- **Exclusions:** tree work requiring climbing, rigging, cranes/lifts, or work near utility lines; roofing repairs beyond emergency tarping; structural/foundation repair; mold, sewage, or biohazard remediation; gas-line work; appliance parts warranties; pest control; landscaping/lawn care; damage requiring licensed specialty trades beyond coordination; pre-existing conditions discovered but not caused during service.
- **Response limitations:** all response targets are subject to stated service area, crew availability, weather, and site safety. Active utility hazards are referred to the utility provider or qualified specialty contractors before PTTR work begins.

**Why this doesn't undermine Recruit:** a Reserve member who uses 4 labor hours in a month pays $129 + $340 = $469 (plus dispatch fees). Recruit delivers the same 4 hours for $265 with rollover. The moment a client averages ~2 hours of labor per month, Recruit is clearly cheaper — Reserve is priced to convert steady users upward, not to trap them at the bottom.

## Exclusions (Scope A and B)

- Full tree removal, felling, or take-down of the standing tree
- Stump grinding or root removal
- **Any limb in contact with, or within 10 ft of, electrical service lines or utility drops** — requires coordination with the utility provider or a qualified/insured tree-service contractor before work begins. PTTR will assess from the ground and make the referral; PTTR does not perform energized-line clearance.
- Structural or arborist health assessment of the lightning-struck tree (recommended — lightning damage can create delayed failure; PTTR can refer a certified arborist)
- Crane, bucket-truck, or lift rental; climbing or rigging work
- Roof damage inspection or repair (beyond emergency tarping if found)
- Structural crack repair — cracks showing displacement, widening, water intrusion, or foundation movement are documented and referred for structural evaluation; only cosmetic patching is included
- Full-wall or whole-room repainting (touch-up only)
- Concealed damage discovered after work begins (change-order with client approval before proceeding)

# 6. Why the Revised Offer Should Convert Better

1. **It sells what the client already said they want.** RC Journeys stated their biggest need three times: emergency response. The old program made them choose between full plans and nothing; Reserve is a direct yes to their stated need at service-call-friendly money.
2. **The immediate job does the selling.** The tree-and-crack quote shows ~$135 of member savings on this week's work alone — the membership's first month pays for itself on a job they already need, converting an abstract subscription pitch into arithmetic.
3. **It removes the forecasting objection instead of arguing with it.** No labor hours means no booking forecast required. The commitment question ("can we predict our hours?") is replaced with an insurance question ("do things break unexpectedly?") — which they answered themselves with the lightning strike.
4. **The upgrade path is built into the math.** At $129 + $85/hr, any client averaging ~2+ labor hours per month saves by moving to Recruit. Reserve members generate exactly the usage data RC Journeys says they lack — after a few months, PTTR can show them their own numbers and let the ladder do the upselling.
5. **The core program is protected.** Recruit through Commander are untouched, remain strict supersets of Reserve, and every rate stays on one consistent ladder ($95 → $85 → $75 → included), so no tier cannibalizes another.
6. **Trust language converts risk-averse hosts.** Explicit definitions (response ≠ completion), honest utility-line and arborist referrals, and clear exclusions read as professionalism — for a host whose asset earns reviews, credibility is the feature.
